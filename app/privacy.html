Privacy

Last updated 14 November 2026

tyro.cell is a free website for practising spreadsheet formulas. This page says plainly what it stores and what it doesn't. You can use everything without an account; signing in with Google is optional.

What stays in your browser

Your progress lives only in your own browser (its local storage): the exercises you've completed, your work on each sheet, the days you practised, and small settings such as whether you've seen the intro tour. Unless you sign in (below), none of it is sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it.

If you use Export progress, the backup file is saved to your device and stays with you. If you use Continue on laptop on a phone, the link holds your completed exercise ids and practice dates after the # sign. That part of a link is never sent to any server; it travels only where you send it.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional and only used to keep your progress in step across devices. Google tells us your name, email address and profile picture link; we don't get your password or anything else from your Google account.

We then store, linked to your account: the exercises you've completed (and whether the solution was open), and the dates you practised. Nothing you type into a sheet is stored in your account. This data is kept by Supabase, our database provider, in its Mumbai (India) region, and access rules ensure only you can read or change your rows.

To delete your account and everything linked to it, use "Report a problem" or message us on LinkedIn and we'll remove it. A self-service delete button is coming. Signing out keeps your progress in that browser.

What we don't collect

No ads, no analytics or tracking scripts, and no advertising cookies. Signing in stores a login token in your browser so you stay signed in; that's the only cookie-like data. Without signing in, no names or email addresses are collected.

Services the site relies on

  • Vercel hosts the website. Like any web host, it may log technical details of each request (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability. See Vercel's privacy policy.
  • Google provides the "Sign in with Google" button and handles sign-in, under Google's privacy policy. To show the button, your browser loads it from Google, which receives your IP address.
  • Supabase stores signed-in progress (Mumbai region). See Supabase's privacy policy.
  • jsDelivr serves the sign-in library to your browser, which sends it your IP address like any download.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces. Your browser downloads them from Google, which receives your IP address when it does. See Google's privacy policy.
  • LinkedIn: the footer link opens LinkedIn's own site, under its own policy.

If you email us

"Report a problem" opens your own email app with the exercise and your answer filled in. If you send it, we receive your email address and message, and use them only to reply and fix the exercise.

Changes

If we add analytics or anything else that collects data, we will update this page before it goes live and say what changes.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use "Report a problem" on any exercise, or message us on LinkedIn.
