How it works

01

Pick an exercise

Structured courses take you from SUM to XLOOKUP. Each exercise mirrors a real job scenario.

02

Write the formula

Same keys as Excel — arrow-key references, F4 for $-locks, Tab to confirm, Ctrl+D to fill down.

03

Get instant feedback

Right or wrong in one click. When you're off, the diagnostic tells you exactly why — not just "incorrect."

Common questions

Is this free?

Yes, completely free right now. No credit card, no trial expiry.

Do I need Excel installed?

No. Tyro.cell runs entirely in your browser — desktop, laptop, or tablet. Nothing to download.

Will my progress be saved?

Right now progress stays in your browser session. Google sign-in is coming soon so it persists across devices.

How is this different from YouTube tutorials?

You write every formula yourself in a real spreadsheet grid. No watching, no pausing, no "I'll try it later." You either get it right or you get a specific diagnostic telling you why not.
