# Vibranium — Formula Drill Library

**116 exercises across 13 files**, covering **38 functions**. All use Indian finance contexts. Not yet integrated into the app — definitions are in the `EX.xxx` format used by `app/index.html`.

To integrate: copy EX definitions into the `EX={}` block in `app/index.html` and add exercise IDs to the `COURSES` modules array.

## Tiers

| Tier | Focus | Exercises | Files |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lookups & multi-criteria aggregation | 28 | vlookup, xlookup, indexmatch, iferror, countifs, averageifs |
| 2 | Text cleanup, logic, horizontal lookup | 24 | text-cleanup, abs-and-or-hlookup |
| 3 | Finance rewrites of generic exercises | 16 | if-rewrites |
| 4 | Remaining engine-ready functions | 48 | power-cagr, rounding, case-textjoin, dates, math, stats, logic-types |

## Difficulty spread

35 easy · 39 medium · 42 hard — much harder skew than the current 45 app exercises (23E/17M/5H).

## What's NOT covered

Financial functions (NPV, IRR, PMT, etc.) — engine support added 2026-08-05, exercises still need authoring.
